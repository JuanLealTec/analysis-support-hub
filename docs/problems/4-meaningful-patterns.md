# 4. I Cannot Identify Meaningful Patterns

## This May Happen When
* Information appears random or disconnected.
* Large datasets make it difficult to notice trends.
* You have reviewed the information several times but no meaningful patterns emerge.

## Questions to Ask Yourself
* Do any trends, similarities, or differences appear repeatedly?
* Have I compared groups, categories, or time periods?
* Have I organized the information in different ways?
* What information appears most frequently?

## Possible Approaches
* **Comparative Analysis.** Compare groups, categories, conditions, or time periods to identify similarities, differences, and trends.
* **Pattern Exploration.** Search for recurring characteristics, frequencies, trends, clusters, or regularities within the information.
* **Visual Analysis.** Represent information visually to make patterns, concentrations, differences, and trends easier to recognize.

??? tip "Support Frameworks & Techniques"
    * Frequency Analysis
    * Group Comparisons
    * Pareto Analysis
    * Segmentation
    * Thematic Analysis (Braun & Clarke)
    * Trend Analysis
