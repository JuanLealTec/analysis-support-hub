# 1. My Information is Incomplete, Inconsistent, or Unreliable

## This May Happen When
* Different files or records contain conflicting information.
* Key information is missing from the available records.
* You suspect errors or inconsistencies in the available information.

## Questions to Ask Yourself
* Are important data or records missing?
* Can the information be verified?
* What information appears incomplete?
* What information appears inconsistent?

## Possible Approaches
* **Data Cleaning.** Review and improve the quality of available information by identifying errors, duplicates, inconsistencies, and missing information.
* **Data Quality Review.** Evaluate whether the available information is complete, consistent, accurate, and appropriate for the project.
