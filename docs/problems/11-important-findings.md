# 11. I Have Many Findings and Do Not Know Which Matter Most

## This May Happen When
* Multiple findings appear relevant.
* Several findings have been developed from the analysis.
* You are unsure which findings deserve greater attention.

## Questions to Ask Yourself
* Which findings contribute most to understanding the project?
* Which findings deserve communication or further development?
* Which findings are most relevant to the project objective or question?
* Which findings are supported by the strongest evidence?

## Possible Approaches
* **Impact Assessment.** Evaluate how much each finding contributes to understanding the project, answering the project question, or explaining the observed results.
* **Prioritization.** Determine which findings deserve greater attention based on relevance, evidence strength, impact, or significance.

??? tip "Support Frameworks & Techniques"
    * Criteria-Based Ranking
    * Impact Analysis
    * Pareto Analysis
    * Significance Comparison
    * So-What? Framework
