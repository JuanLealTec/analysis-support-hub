# 10. I Do Not Understand Why This May Be Happening

## This May Happen When
* A pattern exists, but the reasons behind it are uncertain.
* An observation or finding has been identified, but its causes remain unclear.
* Multiple factors may be contributing to the same result.

## Questions to Ask Yourself
* Are there alternative explanations?
* What factors may contribute to this result?
* What possible causes should be considered?
* Which causes appear most consistent with the evidence?

## Possible Approaches
* **Comparative Analysis.** Compare groups, conditions, categories, or situations to identify factors associated with an observed result.
* **Alternative Explanation Analysis.** Generate and evaluate multiple possible causes before selecting the most plausible explanation.
* **Root Cause Analysis.** Investigate underlying causes that may contribute to an observed result.
