# 7. I Do Not Understand What an Observation May Mean

## This May Happen When
* Several possible explanations seem reasonable.
* You identify a pattern but do not know what it suggests.
* You notice a difference between groups but cannot explain it.

## Questions to Ask Yourself
* Does the available evidence support this interpretation?
* What alternative interpretations should be considered?
* What factors may help explain this result?
* What may this observation suggest?

## Possible Approaches
* **Alternative Perspectives.** Explore multiple possible explanations before deciding which interpretation appears strongest.
* **Interpretation Development.** Develop plausible explanations by examining observations within the broader context of the project.
