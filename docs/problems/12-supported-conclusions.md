# 12. I Am Not Sure What Conclusions Can Reasonably Be Supported

## This May Happen When
* Findings have been developed, but conclusions remain uncertain.
* Several conclusions seem possible.
* You are concerned about making claims that exceed the available evidence.

## Questions to Ask Yourself
* Are important limitations being considered?
* Does the conclusion go beyond what the evidence supports?
* Does the conclusion remain faithful to the findings?
* Which findings support this conclusion?

## Possible Approaches
* **Conclusion Validation.** Determine whether conclusions remain consistent with the findings and evidence developed during analysis.
* **Scope and Limitation Review.** Evaluate whether conclusions remain within the boundaries and limitations of the project.

??? tip "Support Frameworks & Techniques"
    * Boundary Checking
    * Conclusion Validation Checklist
    * Finding-to-Conclusion Mapping
    * Limitation Analysis
    * Scope Review
