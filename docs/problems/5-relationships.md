# 5. I Cannot Identify Relationships Between Variables, Categories, or Observations

## This May Happen When
* Information contains multiple categories or factors that may interact.
* Patterns appear to exist, but connections are unclear.
* Variables seem related, but the relationship cannot be explained.

## Questions to Ask Yourself
* Are there relationships that appear consistently across the information?
* Do similar observations share common characteristics?
* What changes when another variable changes?
* Which variables or categories may be connected?

## Possible Approaches
* **Comparative Analysis.** Compare categories, groups, conditions, or time periods to identify possible relationships and connections.
* **Relationship Exploration.** Investigate whether variables, categories, observations, or characteristics appear connected or influence one another.
* **Visual Analysis.** Represent information visually to make relationships, associations, and interactions easier to identify.
