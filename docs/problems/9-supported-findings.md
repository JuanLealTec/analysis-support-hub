# 9. I Am Not Sure Whether a Finding Is Actually Supported

## This May Happen When
* A finding appears reasonable, but supporting evidence is unclear.
* Multiple findings have been developed and require validation.
* You are unsure whether a finding goes beyond the available information.

## Questions to Ask Yourself
* Am I claiming more than the evidence supports?
* Could the evidence support a different finding?
* Does the finding remain faithful to the original observation?
* What evidence supports this finding?

## Possible Approaches
* **Alternative Explanation Analysis.** Evaluate whether alternative explanations could account for the same evidence.
* **Evidence-Based Validation.** Determine whether a finding is directly supported by the available evidence.
* **Scope and Limitation Review.** Evaluate whether a finding remains within the boundaries and limitations of the available information.

??? tip "Support Frameworks & Techniques"
    * Boundary Checking
    * Claim-Evidence Matrix
    * Counterexample Testing
    * Evidence Mapping
    * Finding Validation
    * Limitation Analysis
