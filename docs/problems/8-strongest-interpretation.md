# 8. I Have Several Interpretations and Do Not Know Which Are Strongest

## This May Happen When
* Different explanations seem equally convincing.
* Multiple interpretations appear reasonable.
* You are unsure which interpretation is best supported by evidence.

## Questions to Ask Yourself
* Does any interpretation contradict available evidence?
* Have alternative explanations been considered fairly?
* Which interpretation is most strongly supported by evidence?
* Which interpretations depend on assumptions?

## Possible Approaches
* **Alternative Explanation Analysis.** Evaluate competing interpretations by comparing explanations and actively looking for weaknesses, contradictions, or counterexamples.
* **External Review.** Seek feedback from knowledgeable individuals who may identify strengths, weaknesses, or assumptions that were overlooked.
* **Evidence-Based Validation.** Determine which interpretation is best supported by the available evidence.
