# 6. I Am Not Sure Whether My Information is Trustworthy

## This May Happen When
* Different sources provide conflicting information.
* Information comes from multiple sources of varying quality.
* You are uncertain whether information is accurate, reliable, or credible.

## Questions to Ask Yourself
* Are there reasons to question the reliability of the source?
* Can the information be verified?
* Do multiple sources support the same claim?
* Where did this information come from?

## Possible Approaches
* **Credibility Assessment.** Evaluate whether information appears reliable, accurate, and trustworthy.
* **Verification.** Confirm information using additional evidence, sources, records, or observations.
