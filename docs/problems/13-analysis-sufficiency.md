# 13. I Do Not Know When Analysis Is Sufficient

## This May Happen When
* Additional analysis continues to generate similar findings.
* New analysis produces little additional insight.
* You are uncertain whether further analysis will add meaningful value.

## Questions to Ask Yourself
* Am I continuing analysis without a clear purpose?
* Do the findings adequately explain the observations?
* Have I answered the project question or objective?
* Would additional analysis likely produce meaningful new insights?

## Possible Approaches
* **Prioritization.** Focus attention on the most meaningful findings rather than continuing
unnecessary analysis.
* **Project Alignment Review.** Evaluate whether the analysis adequately addresses the project's objective, question, or purpose.
* **Scope and Limitation Review.** Determine whether additional analysis would meaningfully improve understanding of the project.
