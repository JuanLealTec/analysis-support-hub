# 2. I Have Too Much Information

## This May Happen When
* Information is scattered across multiple files, documents, or sources.
* Large amounts of information feel overwhelming.
* Multiple files contain similar information.
* You are unsure where to begin reviewing the information.

## Questions to Ask Yourself
* Can the information be filtered?
* Can the information be grouped?
* Do I need to review all available information?
* Which information is most relevant to the project?

## Possible Approaches
* **Information Organization.** Organize information into structures that make it easier to review, understand, compare, and analyze.
* **Information Reduction.** Reduce the amount of information being reviewed by focusing on the most relevant, representative, or useful information.

??? tip "Support Frameworks & Techniques"
    * Categorization
    * Filtering
    * Grouping
    * Representative Sampling
    * Sorting
    * Tagging

??? example "Possible Tools"
    * Annotation & Note-Taking Tools
    * Qualitative Coding & Analysis Tools (Taguette, Atlas.ti)
    * Electronic Worksheets (for quantitative data pooling)
    * Mind-Mapping Tools
